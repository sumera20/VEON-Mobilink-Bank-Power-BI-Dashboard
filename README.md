# VEON – Mobilink Bank | Data Analytics & Power BI

## Overview

This portfolio project showcases data analysis and reporting experience developed during my work as a **Data Analyst at VEON – Mobilink Microfinance Bank (MMBL)**.

The work focused on analysing financial and operational data, developing KPIs, and creating reporting solutions to support management decision-making across branch operations.

## Key Areas

* Financial and operational data analysis
* KPI development and monitoring
* Power BI dashboard development
* Excel-based analysis and reporting
* Database-driven reporting
* Data validation and transformation
* Branch-level performance monitoring
* Management reporting and data visualisation

## Banking Analytics Areas

The analysis covered areas including:

* Cash Management
* ATM/CDM Operations
* RTGS
* Clearing
* Suspense Accounts
* Vault Management
* Cash-in-Hand Monitoring
* Operational Exceptions
